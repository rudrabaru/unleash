Entries are never rewritten after this compaction; mark an old one `Superseded by D-xx` instead. ## Open items needing a decision (with data)

- **Phrase check floor** (D-04): stays 0.5 until at least 10 genuine human takes from at least 3 speakers exist.
- **Phrase check is a bag of words:** order is not enforced and extra spoken words are not penalised, although the schema says "in order"; with 4-of-5 slack the effective entropy is about 19 bits, and reading about 40 vocabulary words covers a challenge by chance 2.6% of the time. Recommended fix: word-level alignment (D-10). Needs approval (must-pass change).
- **Fusion fails open:** with no minimum signal coverage, audio plus challenge alone can reach `VERIFIED` if liveness, deepfake and replay error out. Recommended: a minimum-coverage rule that caps the verdict at `SUSPICIOUS`, never `FAILED`. Needs approval.
- **Joint veto and weights of the uncalibrated signals** (deepfake, replay, voice authenticity, lip sync): decide after re-measuring on browser recordings (D-30, D-37).
- **Deepfake model:** one retrain with the same face crop as runtime, success meaning validation at least 0.85, genuine clips below 0.3 fake probability and `fake.webm` flagged; otherwise reported but not scored.
- **Challenge flow (read, speak, act):** show the words and face the camera for about 3 s, say them facing the camera for about 5 s, then the words disappear and the head action is cued for about 4 s. Keeps lip sync usable (the mouth is visible while speaking) and lets the person read. Risks: a longer read window gives a synthetic-voice attacker more time; order is not checked by the backend (checking it would be a new check). Changes the session time budget, not a must-pass threshold. Needs approval; recommended, and the calibration set should be recorded this way.
- **Look up and down:** keep in code, include in the calibration set with a "did not act, just looked at the screen" take for each; the pitch line (10 degrees from the camera) may be passed by a natural slouch, and look up depends on camera position. Drop and keep left, right and blink only if the data shows they are unreliable.
- **Lip sync under head pose and dim light:** recommended: score `None` (weight redistributed) when the lip signal is too flat or the head is turned too far to judge. Changes fusion coverage; needs approval and the calibration set.
- **Hand gesture challenge (candidate):** MediaPipe HandLandmarker (CPU, one more model file); first try "show N fingers" with N random from 1 to 5, spoken first and gestured after. Adds a challenge type to the visual must-pass check, so needs approval. Not measured: reliability in dim light and across hands; does not help against a live person puppeteered with a real hand; a hand over the face costs the other detectors frames. Prototype after the calibration set, compare with the head actions on the same takes.
- **Blink challenge:** follow D-09 (passive blink) when the challenge set changes.
- **Checkpoint distribution:** where the 250 MB checkpoint is published and who records its SHA-256 (D-29).
